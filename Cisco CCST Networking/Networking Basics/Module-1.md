# 📖 Overview

This module introduces the fundamentals of computer networking, explaining how devices communicate, the different types of networks, how data is transmitted, and the concepts of bandwidth and throughput. It also highlights the growing importance of internet-connected devices in everyday life.

---

# 🌍 What is the Internet?

The **Internet** is a global collection of interconnected networks that communicate using common standards. It is not owned by any single person or organization. Instead, thousands of local networks work together to exchange information through telephone lines, fiber-optic cables, wireless connections, and satellite links.

Today, the internet connects people worldwide through services such as email, social media, video conferencing, online gaming, and cloud-based applications.

---

# 🏠 Types of Networks

Networks come in different sizes depending on their purpose.

### Small Home Networks
Connect a few devices like laptops, smartphones, printers, and smart TVs for internet access and resource sharing.

### SOHO Networks (Small Office/Home Office)
Used by small businesses and home offices to share printers, files, and internet connectivity.

### Medium to Large Networks
Support hundreds or thousands of devices within organizations, enabling communication, centralized storage, and business operations.

### World Wide Networks
Large-scale networks that connect multiple local networks across cities and countries through the internet.

# 💾 Data Transmission

Computers communicate using **binary digits (bits)**, where each bit has only two possible values:

- **0**
- **1**

A group of **8 bits** forms **1 Byte**.

Characters, numbers, images, audio, and videos are all converted into binary before being processed or transmitted. One common encoding standard is **ASCII**, where every character is represented by eight bits.

---

# 📡 Methods of Data Transmission

Data travels across network media using signals. The three common transmission methods are:

### Electrical Signals
Data is transmitted as electrical pulses through copper cables.

### Optical Signals
Electrical signals are converted into light pulses and transmitted through fiber-optic cables.

### Wireless Signals
Data is transmitted using radio waves, microwaves, or infrared without physical cables.

---

# ⚡ Bandwidth vs Throughput

### Bandwidth
Bandwidth refers to the **maximum amount of data** that can be transmitted over a network in a given amount of time.

### Throughput
Throughput is the **actual amount of data successfully transferred** over the network.

Throughput is usually lower than bandwidth due to:

- Network traffic
- Type of transmitted data
- Number of network devices
- Latency

**Latency** is the delay experienced while data travels from the sender to the receiver.
